QC-1

The QR is the doorway, not the warehouse.

Pebble keeps public QR codes simple and predictable. The code points to a short trusted route. The intelligence stays in the backend.

That lets the same familiar interaction support contact, help, return, verification, check-in and other useful actions without turning the printed QR into a dense block of data.

One code. One clear purpose. One trusted route.

Built around restraint.

Just because technology can collect more does not mean it should.

Minimum data

Collect only what is needed for the stated purpose.

No unnecessary tracking

Pebble is not designed to build movement or behaviour profiles.

Clear purpose

The person scanning should know why they are scanning and what happens next.

Human choice

Use the technology when you need it. Leave it alone when you do not.

Quiet until useful.

Pebble is designed to sit quietly in the background. No engagement hooks. No attention capture. No requirement to keep opening an app just to prove the service has value.

Activate it once. Let it sit quietly. If help is ever needed, Pebble is there.
